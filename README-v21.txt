GEC Platform v21
- Team approvals show crests and can be accepted/rejected by Staff.
- Team crest uploads are served from the persistent upload directory.
- Team owners can send Free Agent offers with a message.
- Players see offers in TRANSFERS and can accept/decline.
- Accepted transfers move the player to the new roster and deactivate the Free Agent listing.
- Transfer blackout windows also block Free Agent offers/applications.
- Cups support registration/unregistration, cup detail pages, Staff draws, results and next-round draws, including byes.
- Existing SQLite data is preserved with additive migrations.
